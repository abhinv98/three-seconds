# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: agency and brand reviewers in India who approve creator deliverables (Reels and Shorts) before they go live. They check several ads a day, scan fast, and need a decisive answer with measured evidence they can cite back to the creator. Creators read the same report as a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Three Seconds checks a creator ad against the ASCI influencer guidelines before it is published: the verdict, the measured evidence with timestamps, the cited clause and the fix. Success is a reviewer approving or sending back an ad in under a minute, with evidence they trust.

## Positioning

VAR for creator ads: a calm, frame-accurate replay booth that measures the disclosure and makes the call.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, forensic, calm. Talks like a match official: specific, neutral, never scolding. Leads with what happened, then the rule. Every claim about time is a number with a unit.

## Anti-references

Web-card SaaS dashboards (boxed cards with shadows, pill tags, gradient blobs). Compliance tools that hedge ("your disclosure might be non compliant"). Celebratory scorecards ("100% compliant!"). Raw blue underlined links.

## Design Principles

- Decision first: the verdict word and one measured sentence, then evidence, then the fix.
- Show the measurement: seconds with one decimal, mm:ss.t timecodes in mono, every timecode seeks the video.
- Every state has a word and an icon; colour is the third signal, never the only one.
- The fix is part of the verdict: no FAIL or REVIEW without one.
- Honest about uncertainty: unverified clauses and thresholds say so plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in both themes (Paper and Ink). The timeline is a keyboard slider (arrows 0.1s, Shift 1s, Home and End); every span, marker and check is reachable by keyboard with a visible 2px focus ring. Works from 360px wide, respects reduced motion, and sets `lang` on transcript lines (hi-Latn, hi-Deva, en) so screen readers switch voice. Mukta renders Latin and Devanagari at the same weight and baseline.
